Privacy policy: planner.cal and news.fm

planner.cal and news.fm are personal tools that Nurassyl Serikov built for his own use. They are part of nurasik14.github.io/nurasik14. Only accounts he owns sign in to them.

  • Google Calendar (planner.cal): it reads and writes events in the owner's calendar from his own laptop.
  • Gmail, read-only (news.fm): it reads the newsletters in a dedicated inbox and turns them into short news summaries. The summaries are encrypted before they are published on this site, and no one can read them without the owner's passphrase. Email bodies, subjects and sender addresses are never published.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's laptop. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No Google data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The newsletter text is processed by Anthropic's Claude to write the summaries. The summaries are read aloud with Microsoft's text-to-speech service.

To revoke access, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: nurasylserikov4@gmail.com
